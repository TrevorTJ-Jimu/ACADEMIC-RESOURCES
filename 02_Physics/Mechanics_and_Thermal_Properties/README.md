## 📌 Module Overview

**Physics I — Mechanics and Thermal Properties of Matter** introduces the fundamental laws governing motion, forces, energy, and heat. It forms the bedrock for all engineering, biomedical, and physical science programmes.

## 📐 Key Equations

```
Kinematics:          v = u + at,   s = ut + ½at²,   v² = u² + 2as

Newton's 2nd Law:    F = ma

Work:                W = F·d·cosθ

Kinetic Energy:      KE = ½mv²

Ideal Gas Law:       PV = nRT

1st Law of Thermo:   ΔU = Q − W

Torque:              τ = r × F = Iα
```
