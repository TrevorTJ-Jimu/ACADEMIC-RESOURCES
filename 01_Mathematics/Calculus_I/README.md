## 📌 Module Overview

**Calculus I** is the gateway to advanced mathematics and engineering. It develops the rigorous tools of **differential and integral calculus**, building intuition for rates of change, areas under curves, and the fundamental theorem connecting them.

## 📐 Key Formulas

```
Limit Definition of Derivative:   f'(x) = lim[h→0] (f(x+h)−f(x)) / h

Power Rule:                        d/dx [xⁿ] = n·xⁿ⁻¹

Chain Rule:                        d/dx [f(g(x))] = f'(g(x))·g'(x)

Fundamental Theorem of Calculus:   ∫[a→b] f(x)dx = F(b) − F(a)

Substitution:                      ∫f(g(x))g'(x)dx = ∫f(u)du
```
