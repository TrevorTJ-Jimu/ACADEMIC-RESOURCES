## 📌 Module Overview

**Complex Analysis** studies functions of complex variables — one of the most elegant and powerful branches of mathematics. It underpins **signal processing, quantum mechanics, fluid dynamics, control systems**, and many areas of pure mathematics.

## 📐 Key Results

```
Euler's Formula:          e^(iθ) = cos θ + i sin θ

Cauchy-Riemann:           ∂u/∂x = ∂v/∂y  and  ∂u/∂y = −∂v/∂x

Cauchy Integral Formula:  f(a) = (1/2πi) ∮_C f(z)/(z−a) dz

Residue Theorem:          ∮_C f(z)dz = 2πi · Σ Res(f, aₖ)

Laurent Series:           f(z) = Σ aₙ(z−z₀)ⁿ  (n from −∞ to ∞)
```
